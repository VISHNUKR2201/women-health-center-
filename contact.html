Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers regarding consultations, emergency arrivals, insurance coverage, and appointment policies at Curevo Salem.

We reserve dedicated same-day walk-in emergency slots across every morning and afternoon clinic session. If you are experiencing acute pain or bleeding, you will be seen immediately by our on-duty senior clinical registrar.

Curevo Salem is empaneled with Star Health, Care Health, HDFC ERGO, ICICI Lombard, Medi Assist, Vidal Health, Paramount TPA, and all major corporate insurance networks for 100% cashless delivery and surgical admissions.

No referral is required. You can self-schedule an appointment directly via this website, over phone call, or as a walk-in patient at our MMR Complex reception desk.

Our 24/7 Labor Triage Suite is always active with resident obstetricians, anesthetists, and neonatal nurses on site. Proceed directly to our dedicated emergency ramp entrance at MMR Complex or call +91 427 8899 101 for immediate ambulance dispatch.

All consultation suites feature soundproofed acoustic partitioning, private examination rooms with female nurse chaperones, and 256-bit encrypted digital electronic health records accessible only by your treating doctor.
